# Step 03 – Scaling & Performance

## Goals
- Scale infrastructure to handle more strategies, symbols, and workloads.
- Harden pipelines for high availability.

## Tasks
- ☐ Horizontal scale of grid-search/ML workers (Celery/RQ clusters, autoscaling).
- ☐ Optimize data pipelines (incremental updates, caching layers, queue backpressure).
- ☐ Implement alerting/on-call rotation and incident response tooling.

## Deliverables
- Scalable backend capable of handling increased load without degradation.
- Operational processes to maintain uptime and performance.
