# Step 01 – Live Trading Readiness

## Goals
- Ensure safe transition from paper to live IBKR execution.
- Put guardrails (throttles, circuit breakers) in place.

## Tasks
- ☐ Implement order throttling and circuit breakers around live execution flows.
- ☐ Build runbooks and checklists for go-live, rollback, and incident response.
- ☐ Add redundancy/failover for broker connectivity.
- ☐ Enable Prometheus/Grafana dashboards for live metrics.

## Deliverables
- Documented, tested procedures for enabling live trading.
- Monitoring and safeguards to halt trading on anomalies.
