# Step 05 – Compliance & Auditability

## Goals
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit trails.
- Capture decision rationale and model provenance for every trade.

## Tasks
- ☐ Store immutable audit logs (strategy, params, signals, approvals) for each live order.
- ☐ Track model versions + data lineage used in decision-making.
- ☐ Implement access controls and data retention policies.
- ☐ Prepare compliance reports (daily/monthly) as required.

## Deliverables
- Comprehensive audit trail covering trades and model decisions.
- Compliance reporting processes aligned with regulatory needs.
